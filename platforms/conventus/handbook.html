The Conventus Handbook

How events flow through Conventus — from the first card to the final report. Short, practical, no jargon.

The event lifecycle

DRAFT→ 📅 SAVE THE DATE→ ✉ REGISTRATION OPEN→ EXECUTION→ CLOSURE

You never set the stage by hand — it follows your actions. Publish the card and invitees can add the date to their calendars; open registration when the invitation is ready; the event runs; after the end date it moves to closure (reports, certificates, documents).

Event Manager — quickstart

1

Enter your Workspace. Conventus → Event Manager door. You only ever see your own events. Pick one or create a new one.

2

Build the event card. In the Engine panel: title, dates, city, summary, point of contact — the who/what/where/when of your event.

3

Publish — Save the Date. The card goes live on the Events page with an 📅 Add-to-Calendar button. Registration stays closed.

4

Open registration. When your invitation is ready, one click opens the registration form. Approve applications from the Applications screen.

5

Run it. Agenda, announcements and Go & Meet travel services all work from the same panel — and everything you publish appears instantly on the public pages.

The four doors

🌍 Go & Meet — the public showcase: browse events, save dates, register, and plan accommodation, transfers and tours from contracted providers.

🎟 Participant / Invitee — your personal event home: registrations, programmes and your travel plan.

🗂 Event Manager — the Organizer Workspace and Studio described above.

🤝 Service Provider — hotels, transfer companies and tour agencies apply, get accredited by Conventity, then manage listings and answer participant requests.

Good to know

🔒 Isolation by design. Every organizer sees only their own events, applications and agendas — enforced at the database level, not just the interface.

📴 Undo is always cheap. Unpublish returns a card to draft; pausing registration returns it to Save-the-Date. Nothing is deleted.

🌙 Your theme and language follow you across every Conventus page.

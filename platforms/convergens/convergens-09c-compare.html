Reading this comparison

Absolute totals reflect crisis scale and population — Sudan's needs are the largest, but coverage percentages show how well each appeal is resourced relative to its own ask. Figures come from different plan vintages and methodologies (see each country snapshot for sources and dates), so treat cross-country differences as indicative, not precise. Sector panels in the individual snapshots use appeal-requirement scale, not people-in-need, where per-sector PiN was unavailable.
